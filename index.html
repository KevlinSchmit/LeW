Every cup has a story.

The best seat at the counter is the one you stop rushing past. Stay long enough and you notice how the glaze catches the light, how the plates keep time, and how the tea changes color as it settles.

I built this counter around those small moments. I like making digital places that feel as though someone has just stepped away from them. Pull up a seat. There’s always a little more to notice.
